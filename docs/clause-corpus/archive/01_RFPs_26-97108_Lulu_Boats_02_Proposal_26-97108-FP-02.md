**Project Details**

The Project is known as Lulu Island Dhows and is located in Lulu Island, Abu Dhabi, U.A.E. Comprises 3 no. fibreglass display dhows [nominal 10 m, 13 m and 16 m], fabricated as heritage-style PROPS, timber topsides, masts and decorative sails; permanently moored, block-anchored ~30 m off the shoreline; each dhow provisioned with [proposed] 3 x 32 A single-phase marine-grade power.

**Packages**

This Proposal includes the following technologies:
- Specialist Lighting
- Local Power *(new package added in this revision, not present in FP-01)*

**Areas**

Scope includes: Lighting - feature lighting design for the 3 dhows (hull grazing, mast/rigging accents, decorative-sail illumination, deck glow, waterline treatment), DMX lighting control system design, coordination with marine electrical/cabling contractor and set-builder; Local Power - design and proposal for solar / battery based power system as requested [sub-contracted work to specialist].

Scope excludes: Lighting - electrical engineering, physical installation/construction, dhow prop/hull/sail fabrication, power infrastructure (the 3 x 32 A supplies), structural/marine engineering, marine navigation lighting and statutory/marine-authority or environmental approvals.
