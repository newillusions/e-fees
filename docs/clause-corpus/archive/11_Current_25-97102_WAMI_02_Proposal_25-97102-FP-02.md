## Design Phase Notes

### Stage 1 - Information Gathering and Initial Report

Specified deliverables include:
- Review all lighting designs (drawings, specifications, reports) within the Wynn resort building, external areas and Island 3 infrastructure and identify any additional information required.
- Identify the lighting control systems specified for each area and suggest appropriate technologies where not specified, and advise suitability for the application and analysis of building wide lighting control system integrations.
- Identify where lighting zones and control groups have been specified and highlight relevant areas where these have not been specified.
- Identify locations of lighting control panels, centralised workstation and connectivity requirements. Identify Lighting control UI and integrations with AV systems and AV UI.
- Prepare and present initial report.

Recommended additional scope elements:
- Initial Reviews and Comments on fixture control grouping.
- Initial Reviews and Comments on lighting zone interfaces and potential issues or design scope gaps.
- Conduct initial discussions with each Lighting Design team to develop an understanding of their overall intent and strategy.
- Develop outline of final report in conjunction with the Client.
- Review agreed scope and staffing to confirm validity.
- Provide Comments and Recommendations for future Stages of the Works

---

## Design Phase Notes

### Stage 2 - Report and Recommendations

In this design phase, the design is developed with a primary focus on achieving the ideas presented in the Concept Design phase.

Specified deliverables include:
- Develop sitewide maps showing the limits of each lighting control system and interfaces / overlaps with the adjacent lighting systems, and update as additional lighting designs become available
- Develop matrix showing interfaces and interdependencies between lighting control system, and update as additional lighting designs become available
- Provide guidance to the design team / contractor where the lighting control system has not been defined within the project design.
- Develop a site wide lighting control strategy documents detailing requirements for each system and recommend options for centralised monitoring and control of area specific lighting control systems including automated control and manual override.
- Support the design teams in development of lighting scenes and obtaining client approval.
- Where areas are under design development, advise lighting designers of similar fitting used in other areas for procurement gains and reduced attic stock.
- Update initial report to include any new information and above noted deliverables and make recommendations as noted, and present.

Recommended additional scope elements:
- Identify integration points with other systems as required
- Additional Reviews and Comments on fixture control grouping.
- Additional Reviews and Comments on lighting zone interfaces and potential issues or design scope gaps.
- Conduct additional discussions with each Lighting Design team to clarify any outstanding issues [as required].
- Review agreed scope and staffing to confirm validity.
- Provide Comments and Recommendations for future Stages of the Works
