**Project Details**

The Project is known as Lulu Island Dhows and is located in Lulu Island, Abu Dhabi, U.A.E.
The Project is a proposed new development and currently comprises of:
- 3 no. fibreglass display dhows [nominal 10 m, 13 m and 16 m], fabricated as heritage-style PROPS [not seaworthy vessels], with timber topsides, masts and decorative sails.
- Permanently moored as a night-time feature, block-anchored ~30 m off the shoreline in shallow water.
- Marine environment: each dhow provisioned with [proposed] 3 x 32 A single-phase marine-grade power.

**Packages**

This Proposal includes the following technologies [referred to as packages or systems]:
- Specialist Lighting

**Areas**

The proposed scope includes the following areas:
- Lighting: Feature lighting design for the 3 dhows (hull grazing, mast/rigging accents, decorative-sail illumination, deck glow, waterline treatment); DMX lighting control system design; coordination with the appointed marine electrical/cabling contractor and the set-builder.

The proposed scope excludes the following areas:
- Lighting: electrical engineering (cable sizing/type, circuitry, distribution, install methods - by the appointed marine electrical/cabling contractor); physical installation and construction (contractor); dhow prop, hull and sail fabrication and fixing provisions (prop builder); power infrastructure (the 3 x 32 A supplies); structural / marine engineering, mooring/anchor design; marine navigation lighting and any statutory/marine-authority or environmental approvals.

## Design Phase Notes

**Preliminaries**

Typical deliverables include: Assistance in defining project aspirations; assistance in project planning and brief development as appropriate; review of site and project information.
emittiv requires the following information in order to commence: any particular requirements or aspirations; any rules and regulations applying to the site; design criteria and standards; operational and functional requirements; program and area allocations; existing project drawings, sketches, models and reports; any established package budgets.

**Stage 1 - Concept Design**

We will prepare a Lighting Concept Design Report for the Project which will provide guidelines for the Client and other Designers to ensure that the Lighting concepts are in line with the overall Project aspirations. Typical deliverables include: attend briefing/design meetings with the Client and design team; conceptual design studies for the feature lighting of the dhows (hull, rigging, sails, deck and the composed group read from shore); low-res 3D renders to convey the design directions; recommended direction; produce presentation materials. We have allowed for up to one revision of the Concept Design Report.

**Stage 2 - Schematic Design**

Typical deliverables include: attend design workshops / Design Team meetings; carry out further development of package design; prepare preliminary package layouts, detail sketches, mock-up (Dubai - arrange and attend), equipment schedule, control intent, logical control channel schedule, power requirements, responsibility matrix; assist others in checking that the package schemes are within the approved budgets; identify integration points with other systems as required. We have allowed for up to one revision of the Schematic Design Report.

**Stage 3 - Detailed Design**

Typical deliverables include: attend design workshops / Design Team meetings / mock-ups; prepare final package layouts, equipment schedule, details for incorporation into Architect's details, power requirements; assist others with coordination; specify integration points; Design Report.

**Stage 4 - Procurement Support**

This stage will prepare the design documents so they can be issued for tender and finalised by the awarded Contractor for construction from an "Open Tender" perspective. Typical deliverables include: assist the Client to source fixtures and suppliers, review quotes, technical clarifications; supply through emittiv may be possible as a separate agreement (installation excluded - supervision insurance only).

**Notes**

This proposal assumes that the Architect [or other Lead Designer] and Electrical Engineer, maintain their usual role and responsibility for the co-ordination of the design and supervision of the installation on site. Information provided is for use by the Architect and Electrical Engineer in the preparation of the construction / tender documentation. emittiv's drawings, specifications and other information is not to be issued direct to the Contractor as tender or construction information. It is assumed that CAD files / drawings provided for our use will be in a ready to use format and require no work by emittiv to prepare them. Any images prepared by emittiv shall be illustrative in nature. emittiv is not an engineering specialist. All information provided regarding trusses, rigging, lifts or other structural or mechanical elements must be verified by suitably qualified professionals. emittiv is not qualified to determine whether the designs meet structural, safety and / or building code requirements. The exact method of fastening or connection must be verified by the Architect / Structural Engineer.

## Post Contract Phase Notes

**Stage 5 - Construction Supervision**

Typical deliverables include: contract meetings with the Construction and Design Team as considered necessary; review Contractors production information; resolution of site queries; final review of package installation. The Package Contractor shall supply personnel to perform this work in compliance with the local legislation and union agreements.

**Stage 6 - Focussing / Aiming and Scene Setting**

Typical deliverables include: supervision of focusing / aiming of adjustable device; oversee the programming of installed control equipment where applicable. Important notes: carried out following the manufacturer's commissioning of the system and after the Contractor's confirmation of all circuits/cables being correctly installed, labelled and operational; systems can only be focused/aimed and programmed when all devices are installed and fully commissioned.

**Notes**

This proposal assumes that the Architect and Electrical Engineer are to maintain their usual role of co-ordination of the design and supervision of the system installation on site. In the event of emittiv visiting site for meetings or inspections and being unable to complete their work, additional charges will be made as outlined in the Fees section of this Proposal.
