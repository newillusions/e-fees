## Stages (Stage 1 - System Engineering)

We will coordinate with MPME to develop and deliver approved system drawings, models, and documentation.

Deliverables include:
- Attend workshops / Project Team meetings
- Briefing and presentation meetings with the Client
- Assist others to develop deliverable schedules
- Carry out development of package specific 3D Revit Models based on linked files to LOD 400 standards [ongoing works]
- Prepare 3D package calculations [as required]
- Prepare package layouts
- Prepare package equipment schedules [as required]
- Prepare logical control channel schedule
- Prepare package power requirements
- Prepare weight loading information
- Assist others to prepare package detail sketches
- Assist others in checking that the package schemes are within the approved budgets
- Identify integration points with other systems as required

In some instances, MPME has already started developing the required documentation, so emittiv shall review and advise on the ongoing works. The Client shall endeavour to provide any required Revit families to the Consultant.

---

## Stages (Stage 2 - Ongoing Management)

We will coordinate with MPME to update the systems drawings, models, and documentation as the project develops.

Deliverables include:
- Attend workshops / Project Team meetings
- Carry out ongoing development of package design elements in Stage 1
- Carry out ongoing development of package specific 3D layouts based on linked files to LOD 400 standards
- Co-ordinate with other contractors and suppliers as required
- Resolution of site queries
- Finalise integration points with other systems as required

We have allowed for 3.5 days per month in this phase. We expect that more work will be required in the early stages and during the weeks leading up to programming, with a reduced requirement of hours during the construction phase.

---

## Stages (Stage 3 - Programming)

Deliverables include:
- Prepare "square one" layouts for each venue
- Prepare patch and configuration information for each venue
- Supervision of focusing / aiming of adjustable device
- Oversee the programming of installed control equipment

Important Notes:
- This will be carried out following the Client's commissioning of the system and after confirmation of all circuits / cables being correctly installed, labelled and operational.
- The systems can only be focused / aimed and programmed when all the devices are installed i.e. fully operational to all areas as stated within the scope, devices and accessories as specified and control systems, fully commissioned by the contractor and manufacturer.
- emittiv will oversee the scene setting of all package control systems with an engineer from the manufacturer of the systems [provided by the Client].

It is anticipated that preparatory works for this stage shall be completed off-site and comprise part of Stage 2. We have allowed for 3 weeks on-site.
