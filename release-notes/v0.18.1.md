## What's new

- **First-run setup's "Test & Continue" now connects with the settings you just entered.** Previously it only worked after restarting the app once - it now works on the first try.
- **The app waits for the database before declaring itself ready**, fixing empty lists and zero counts that could briefly appear right after starting the app or reconnecting.
- **Project, proposal, company, and contact lists show a real connection error with a Retry button** when a load fails, instead of quietly pretending the list is empty.
