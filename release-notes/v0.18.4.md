## What's new

- **Creating a fee revision is now all-or-nothing.** The new revision and the superseding of the previous one are saved together, so a failure part-way no longer leaves a half-created revision (#68). A cloned revision also carries over pricing, post-contract and reimbursables (#66).
- **Scope Builder is honest about saving and loading.** It shows "Unsaved changes" when your edits are not yet saved, loads your saved scope and wording when you reopen it, and Reset returns to your last save (#69).
- **Library defaults are a separate action** in the Scope Builder, so restoring defaults no longer happens by accident through Reset (#69).
- **Items removed from the library are marked "not in active library" and kept**, rather than silently dropping out of a saved scope. Stage labels are kept as well (#69).
- **Fee updates are validated before they are applied**, so a malformed update is rejected instead of being merged (#67).
- **Proposal flow streamlined** following the UX audit (#63), and scope edits no longer overwrite fields you have curated (#61).
